# AFII Corporate India V3 – Media references and accuracy

**Photography is illustrative only.** It is NOT a documentary photograph of the named AFII transaction, party, office or physical property. We use different image IDs for every distinct expertise, industry and transaction entity; repeated use of the same project image within its own listing and detail page is intentional identity continuity.

The originally authored hero image and subtle motion video are bundled with the patch. The listed 23 third-party photographs are newly selected for V3 and are downloaded to local static assets at installation, with link provenance for legal review. They were not referenced by V2's `site.ts` and `transactions.ts`; this cannot establish that photographs have never appeared anywhere else on the public web.
